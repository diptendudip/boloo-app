For a better experience, you can install it like a real app:

### On Android:
1. Open the link in Chrome
2. Tap menu (⋮) → "Install app"
3. Icon appears on your home screen!

### On iPhone:
1. Open the link in Safari
2. Tap Share (⬆️) → "Add to Home Screen"
3. Icon appears on your home screen!
