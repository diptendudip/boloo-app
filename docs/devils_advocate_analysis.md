- **This is sophisticated prompt engineering, not agentic AI research**

- BKC reviewers know the difference between real agentic systems and chatbot workflows
- Claiming "agentic AI" when you mean "multi-step LLM orchestration" = credibility death

**Option A: Reframe under "AI & Human Experience"**

- From: "We built agentic AI"

✓ Focuses on human-AI interaction (BKC's strength)
