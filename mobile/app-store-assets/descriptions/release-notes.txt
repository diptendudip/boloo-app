VERSION 1.0.0 - Initial Release

Welcome to Boloo - Citizen Reporting!

NEW FEATURES:
• Report civic issues with photo evidence
• Real-time report tracking
• Multi-language support (English & Hindi)
• Location-based issue reporting
• Category-based issue submission
• User profile and history
• Push notifications for updates

CATEGORIES AVAILABLE:
• Potholes and road damage
• Street lighting
• Garbage and sanitation
• Water supply
• Drainage issues
• Public infrastructure
• Illegal dumping
• And more...

We're excited to help you make a difference in your community!

Questions or feedback? Contact us at support@boloo.com

Thank you for downloading Boloo! 🎉

✗ Internal terminology
✗ Bug tracking IDs
